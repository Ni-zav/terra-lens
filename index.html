OUR SCIENTIFIC APPROACH

Questions deserve
evidence, not guesses.

01 / DATA

Observe

Fetch real NASA POWER monthly meteorological series derived from NASA MERRA-2. Grid-cell estimates are not direct local sensor observations.

02 / ANALYSIS

Measure

Aggregate only complete calendar years, fit a least-squares trend, and report the change per decade with a confidence interval.

03 / INFERENCE

Question

Use a two-sided classical t-test with transparent assumptions. Statistical significance is not proof of causation or a forecast.
